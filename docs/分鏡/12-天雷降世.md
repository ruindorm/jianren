# 第十二章　劍關二階段：天雷降世　分鏡

章型：打鬥章　·　格數：2　·　規範：[分鏡提示詞規範](../分鏡提示詞規範.md)

> 全書最大的場面。兩格走「量級 → 判定」：置頂格建立規模但不透露勝負，置底格交付那一聲清脆的響聲。

## SB-12-A

| 欄位 | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **位置** | 置頂 |
| **鏡頭目的** | 建立量級。讀者要在翻到這章的第一秒就知道「規模跟前面完全不同了」 |
| **揭示邊界** | **不得洩露勝負**：兩條光柱必須看起來勢均力敵，不得有任何一柱受損或劍歪斜。不得出現劍匣、不得出現龍形 |
| **景別・機位・視線** | 極遠景／高機位俯瞰／人物只剩剪影 |
| **凍結瞬間** | 白光**正從天頂的裂縫往下灌、下緣還沒到地**。兩條光柱之間的空間裂成錯位的層，層與層之間有位移。地面剪影群眾手臂舉起遮眼、身體正在往後仰 |
| **資產綁定** | P05 光柱 ×2（**左柱＝佩劍本尊，右柱＝P02 傲天劍**，兩柱插地深度相同）@ S03。光源：天頂唯一光源，向下 |
| **排除項** | 劍匣、龍形、任何一方倒下、文字 |

**五件事檢查**：誰打誰＝兩柱對峙已明確／空間幾何＝左右分立、高機位交代全局／重量感＝群眾後仰、塵土被壓下／層次＝天／柱／地三層，剪影前景＋光柱中景＋裂縫背景／代價＝地面焦痕與碎石。

### 提示詞

```
16-bit pixel art, SNES-era JRPG cutscene illustration, hard pixel edges,
no anti-aliasing, limited palette of about 24 colors, dithered gradients,
1px dark outlines on characters, 16:9 composition.

Extreme wide shot, high overhead camera looking down across a shattered
plaza, human figures reduced to small silhouettes along the lower edge.

Two tall columns of pale light stand upright far apart, left and right, each
with a straight sword planted in the ground inside it at the same depth. The
space between them is split into misaligned horizontal layers, each layer
offset sideways from the one below.

White lightning is pouring down through a tear in the sky right now, its
leading edge still partway down, not yet touching the ground. The silhouetted
figures below are raising their arms across their eyes, bodies tilting
backward. Dust is being pressed flat outward from the centre.

Scorched flagstones, cracked stone, the tear overhead as the only light
source casting everything downward.

Exclude: any casket or box, dragon shapes, any figure fallen or losing,
lettering.
```

## SB-12-B

| 欄位 | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **位置** | 置底 |
| **鏡頭目的** | 交付判定。全章的勝負濃縮成一個物理事實：劍離開了光柱 |
| **揭示邊界** | 可含勝負。**不得含劍匣、不得含龍傲天回頭的眼神、不得含意識回歸後的任何人**——那些都在這一瞬之後 |
| **景別・機位・視線** | 特寫／平視／畫框只裝得下劍柄與光柱邊界 |
| **凍結瞬間** | 傲天劍**已離開光柱內壁一吋、正在往外飛**，劍尖仍在柱內、劍柄已出柱外。柱壁在穿出點碎成六角光片、光片正在往外散。畫面上方一道分形劍陣的細線接進光柱 |
| **資產綁定** | P02 傲天劍（穿出中）+ P05 右柱（穿出點碎裂）+ 御劍陣 2 分形細線（自上方接入）@ S03 |
| **排除項** | 人物臉部、劍匣、龍形、背景群眾 |
| **可選大字** | 右下角可放 `YOU LOSE`，標題級大字。兩個短英文字，符合文字政策。生成後逐字檢查 |

**五件事檢查**：誰打誰＝分形細線指明來源／空間幾何＝柱壁內外的穿出方向明確／重量感＝碎裂光片的擴散／層次＝柱內／柱壁／柱外三層／代價＝碎裂的柱壁本身。

### 提示詞

```
16-bit pixel art, SNES-era JRPG cutscene illustration, hard pixel edges,
no anti-aliasing, limited palette of about 24 colors, dithered gradients,
1px dark outlines on characters, 16:9 composition.

Extreme close-up, eye level, the frame filled by a sword hilt and the curved
inner wall of a column of light.

An ornate dark sword has just broken through the wall of the light column and
is travelling outward: its tip is still inside the column, its hilt already
clear of it, one inch of open air between blade and wall. The wall is
shattering into hexagonal shards at the puncture point and the shards are
flying outward right now.

A thin branching lattice of glowing sword-energy filaments enters the frame
from the top, feeding into the column.

Pale light from the column itself is the only source, casting hard cyan rim
light along the blade.

Exclude: faces, any casket or box, dragon shapes, background crowd.
```
