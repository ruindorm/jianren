# 第八章　吾劍啟動　分鏡

章型：轉折章　·　格數：2　·　規範：[分鏡提示詞規範](../分鏡提示詞規範.md)

> 本章是全書最重要的笑點所在，兩格的分工完全由**規則五（邊界順讀）**決定：
> 置頂格不准洩露愛心，置底格才能交付。這兩格單獨看都成立，合起來是一個完整的落差。

## SB-08-A

| 欄位 | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **位置** | 置頂 |
| **鏡頭目的** | 接住第七章結尾被切斷的「吾名——」，讓讀者感覺「有人接關了」，而且是帶著氣勢接的 |
| **揭示邊界** | 只到宣告本身。**不得出現愛心手勢、不得出現降下的新光柱、不得出現任何人的反應** |
| **景別・機位・視線** | 中近景／略仰角英雄機位／單人。視線正對鏡頭偏左上 |
| **凍結瞬間** | 男主角張口大喝**到一半**，下巴微抬，一手劍指往前推出。佩劍浮在他身前，**劍身正在轉正、劍尖還斜著十五度沒到位**。指示燈由白轉青，光暈剛開始往外擴 |
| **資產綁定** | C01（劍指前推）+ C02（浮空、燈：白轉青、劍尖斜十五度）@ S03。背景：P05 光柱餘光（天羽劍已被打出，柱內空著） |
| **排除項** | 愛心手勢、傲天劍鏡像、紅光眼、任何文字 |

### 提示詞

```
16-bit pixel art, SNES-era JRPG cutscene illustration, hard pixel edges,
no anti-aliasing, limited palette of about 24 colors, dithered gradients,
1px dark outlines on characters, 16:9 composition.

Medium close-up, low hero angle looking up, single figure filling the left
two-thirds of the frame, gaze aimed past the camera to the upper left.

A young swordsman in dark fitted robes with rolled-up sleeves and a loose red
cord at the waist, messy topknot with stray strands. Mouth open mid-shout,
chin lifted, one hand thrust forward in a two-finger sword-pointing gesture.

He is caught halfway through the shout. A plain unadorned straight sword
floats in the air before him, still rotating upright, its tip tilted fifteen
degrees off vertical. The small square indicator gem at its guard is shifting
from white to cyan, its glow just beginning to bloom outward. Dust is still
drifting upward around them.

Fractured otherworldly sky, a tall column of pale light standing empty in the
background, cold rim light from behind.

Exclude: heart-shaped hand gesture, any second sword, red glowing eyes,
lettering.
```

## SB-08-B

| 欄位 | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **位置** | 置底 |
| **鏡頭目的** | 交付全書最大的落差——一個中二到極點的宣告，收在一個羞恥到極點的手勢，而且**所有人都看見了**。反應比動作重要 |
| **揭示邊界** | 可以含愛心與全場反應。**不得含已經降下的新光柱、不得含紅光眼**——那些是笑點之後才發生的 |
| **景別・機位・視線** | 全景群像／平視／男主角在中央偏左、四分之三背向鏡頭。**構圖的重點是把所有旁觀者的臉塞進框裡** |
| **凍結瞬間** | 男主角雙手在胸前合成愛心、脹紅著臉、眼睛閉起。佩劍浮在他正前方，指示燈亮成柔粉。龍傲天、門使、小師妹全部僵住、嘴微張。倒地的大師兄**正撐起半身**，手肘還沒撐直 |
| **資產綁定** | C01（背向、愛心）+ C02（燈：粉）+ C03（倒地撐起）+ C04（僵住）+ C07 + C08 @ S03 |
| **排除項** | 新降下的光柱、紅光眼、鏡像劍、中文字 |
| **可選大字** | 畫面上方可放三個並排的 `?` 作為像素風符號。用了就必須生成後檢查——不確定就不要用 |

### 提示詞

```
16-bit pixel art, SNES-era JRPG cutscene illustration, hard pixel edges,
no anti-aliasing, limited palette of about 24 colors, dithered gradients,
1px dark outlines on characters, 16:9 composition.

Wide group shot, eye level, the main figure centre-left seen from three
quarters behind, every onlooker's face visible in frame.

Centre-left, back to camera: a young swordsman in dark fitted robes with
rolled-up sleeves and a red waist cord, messy topknot, both hands joined into
a heart shape at his chest, face flushed red in profile, eyes squeezed shut.
A plain straight sword floats directly in front of him, the small square
indicator gem at its guard glowing soft pink.

Right: a tall man in black-and-gold dragon-patterned robes with a high crown,
chin lifted, frozen mid-sneer with his mouth slightly open. Behind him, a row
of identically uniformed masked attendants, all motionless. Left: a girl in
pale short robes with trailing ribbons, staring, mouth open. Foreground
ground level: a swordsman in a white blue-trimmed robe with a high ponytail
lying fallen, pushing himself half upright, elbow not yet locked.

Fractured otherworldly sky, scorched flagstones, flat cold light.

Exclude: standing columns of light, red glowing eyes, mirrored swords,
Chinese characters.
```
