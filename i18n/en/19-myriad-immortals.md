# Chapter Nineteen — Myriad Immortals

> **[Within the Echo]**

**Handsome Swordsman**: Damn you all—! **SWORD! COME!**

The sword lying beside the fallen swordswoman flew back into the Handsome Swordsman's hand. Holding two blades at once, he—

**Handsome Swordsman**: **MYRIAD IMMORTALS—!**

From both ends of the Sword Gate space came a sound like the roar of a vast dragon. An unstoppable blade of killing qi came tearing out of the abyss, splitting everything in its path. Target: the Handsome Swordsman.

Holding the mirror of her sword, his face carried shock and fury in equal measure.

As the Myriad Immortals technique unfolded, the space around him rippled as if in resonance, and a white mist rose through it — like the realm of the immortals themselves.

**Handsome Swordsman**: HAAA—!

The killing qi slammed into the Myriad Immortals barrier. Blinding sparks and blade-light scattered in every direction. That unimaginable attack — held back by the body of a single mortal…

But—!

From another direction came a second, sharper blade of qi. The Handsome Swordsman channelled the mirror of her sword and, by main force, blocked that one too—

A trial meant to be withstood by two, now held by one alone.

**Handsome Swordsman**: Yun'er… I… will… hold… (looking down at her body beside him)
