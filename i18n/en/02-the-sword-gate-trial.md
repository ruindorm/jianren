# Chapter Two — The Sword Gate Trial

**Master**: This master does not, in truth, know what a "no cap" is. But remember — a cultivator must not cling to names.

**Junior Sister**: Master~ "No cap" means "that's true"! Just like the teaching Master gave us a moment ago — no cap!

**Male Lead**: Junior Sister's command of language is outstanding!

**Senior Brother**: Enough talk! Junior Brother, if not for your qi deviation, you would have faced me at the Sword Gate long ago.

**Sword**: Master, hurry up and expose him — Senior Brother is the one waiting for Master to finish teaching him the *Myriad Immortals Manual* before he'll have the confidence to beat you. That is the actual reason he won't hold the trial.

**Male Lead**: The hell would you know!

**Senior Brother**: (inwardly: Is he talking to me?)

**Junior Sister**: (tilting her head) (Is baby angry…)

**Master**: (stroking his beard, deep in thought) (This child… could he be trying to enlighten his master?)

**Sword**: Master, shall we proceed with contingency plan two?
