> **[Sword Gate · Third Stage]**

**Yun'er**: Ping — are you all right?

**Yuan Ping**: Yun — when it comes, move to the plan!

**Yun'er**: Ping… you're hurt… let's withdraw, please? I was never going to live long anyway.
