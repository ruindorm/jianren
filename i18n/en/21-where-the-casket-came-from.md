# Chapter Twenty-One — Where the Casket Came From

The space of the main hall convulsed, and the warped pillars and walls slowly returned to what they had been.

One by one, everyone came back to themselves—

**Master**: That. That is where the casket came from.

**Junior Sister**: Master… what about her? Your wife. What happened after you left the Gate?

**My Name**: Holy shit! That casket the Dragonrealm Gate was carrying — it's Master's!

**Master**: Patience…
