# Chapter Twelve — Phase Two: Heaven's Lightning

A terrible sound came from above the inner space; everyone hurried to shut their eyes.

White light, and a rolling roar — and between the space of consciousness and the pillars of light, the world sheared apart in crossing fractures.

The dragon-form slipped through the seam and drove into Long Aotian's body. The **Dragon Convergence Palm** launched at My Name's chest—

Powered by borrowed strength, the palm carried appalling force. My Name sank his weight into his stance, calculating the timing gap between his sword's resistance and his own—

Both men had their eyes closed. The flash and thunder of the heavenly lightning caught My Name between the assault without and the assault within, and the strain was brutal…

> **〔The Outer Space〕**

Between the two pillars, the final exchange was about to land—

My Name (Sword Edition)'s fractal array connected the heavenly lightning directly to the Proudheaven pillar, forming a precision strike that could not be defended.

The Heavenly Dragon Morphing Slash struck the pillar holding My Name's sword, and the blade was driven inch by inch toward the open air—

And then My Name's body, on pure instinct, severed the tail of the slash…

Everyone held their breath…

A single clear ring — and the Proudheaven Sword was blasted out of its pillar first!

**My Name (Sword Edition)**: **YOU LOSE!**

Long Aotian's palm was halfway through its arc… and his consciousness began to dissolve. My Name braced to take the full weight of that strike — but his own consciousness was fading too…

The lightning ceased. The pillars entered the settlement phase—

**Senior Brother and Junior Sister**: WE WON!

But…

**Gate Envoy**: NOW!

Two envoys flung the sword casket open. The Proudheaven Sword was drawn in, and both men's consciousness snapped back at once.

The dimensional space vanished in an instant.

**Master**: Mm. So it does still exist — a forced termination of the Sword Gate… I never expected that casket to remain in the world…

**Senior Brother**: Your Transcendent Realm Sect is beneath contempt!

**Junior Sister**: I've never seen anyone so classless!

Long Aotian did not appear to care in the slightest. His gaze passed over Senior Brother and settled on My Name, pinning him there for a long moment…

Then he turned and left without looking back.

**The Dragonrealm envoys**: Consider yourselves lucky today! We're leaving!

**My Name**: (sitting on the ground, holding his head) Disconnected… ow… my head…

**Junior Sister**: (running over) Baby, are you okay? (taking out a handkerchief to wipe his face)

**My Name**: (seeing the handkerchief, a smug smile spreading) I'm fine. You carry everything I give you?

**Junior Sister**: Of course I do… you just won, you know… (blushing)

**Sword**: Master, your physical reflexes are as excellent as ever! That instantaneous response was extremely cool—

**My Name**: Were you trying to get me killed with that move? I'll deal with you later! Ah — not you, Junior Sister, I didn't mean you…
