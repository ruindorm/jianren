# Chapter Four — Dragons on the Front Hall

**Male Lead**: What the hell? What is this?

**Junior Sister**: Didn't you Dragonrealm people come to challenge the Sword Gate? Why have you turned our front hall into *this*?

**Senior Brother**: You carved dragons into our floor and our pillars?

**Gate Envoy**: Haha — we were here, so we left a few marks. Wouldn't you like to admire them?

**Senior Brother**: Insolence! You didn't come to challenge the Sword Gate. You came to strut.

**Gate Envoy**: We'll take both!

**Male Lead**: Bastard! Hey! Get out there!

**Sword**: Master, are you calling me? My name is not "Hey." Would you like to modify the activation command?

**Gate Envoy**: Your sect really does breed qi deviation! The enemy is at your gate and he's still muttering to himself?

**Junior Sister**: Senior Brothers, don't be rash. Let me. (stepping forward)

**Junior Sister**: I've heard the dragon-blades of the Dragonrealm Gate are devastating, and elegant besides. But the dragons you carved look like maggots.

**Gate Envoy**: What did you say!

**Junior Sister**: Don't rush. For one sect to challenge another's Sword Gate, the swordsman and his sword must be separated and held in escrow, every technique must be disclosed in full, and the loser must have their defeat carved in shame upon their own Sword Gate. Which is precisely why sects don't go around challenging each other…

**Gate Envoy**: Hmph! We know the rules better than you do. That is exactly what we came here for today — to carve your sect's shame into your own Sword Gate!

**Sword**: Master, now would be a good moment to explain further. The audience will follow it more easily.

**Male Lead**: You want me to sell you for scrap? Full of ideas now — where were you when I told you to get out there?

**Sword**: They have not yet consented to escrow. I cannot enter early. Also, you have not issued the activation com—

**Male Lead**: Does the activation have to be *now*?

**Sword**: It is somewhat humiliating, yes. But Master configured it himself.

**Male Lead**: Ugh…
