# Chapter Seven — Heavenly Dragon Slash

> **〔The Outer Space〕**

**Junior Sister**: Ah—!

Senior Brother was trying to break free of Long Aotian and press on toward the Proudheaven pillar — and Long Aotian seemed to be waiting for exactly that.

Senior Brother unleashed the **Twin Plume Strike**, firing sword-qi forward and backward at once: one blade of it to hold off Long Aotian, one to strike the Proudheaven Sword—

The Proudheaven Sword took the blow and lifted half an inch. One more strike and it would be out of the pillar. But…

**"HEAVENLY DRAGON SLASH—!"**

An overwhelming edge of sword-qi came from behind him. Senior Brother, rushing to force the Proudheaven Sword out, took it full on — hurled dozens of metres and left where he fell…

Long Aotian, seeing his opening, walked toward the Skyplume pillar with something close to mockery — and deliberately, with the clumsy motion of a man who knew no martial arts at all, knocked the Skyplume Sword out of its pillar.

**Gate Envoy**: Haha! Not worth the effort! Your sect is a joke!

**Junior Sister**: Senior Brother…

The dimensional space began to warp. And just as the pillars were about to withdraw—

**Male Lead**: (bellowing) THAT IS ENOUGH! My name—
