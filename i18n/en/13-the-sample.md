# Chapter Thirteen — The Sample

**Long Aotian**: Sect Master. The mission was a success.

**Sect Master**: Good. Open it.

**Gate Envoy**: (carrying in the casket and opening it)

**Sect Master**: (raising the Proudheaven Sword and a second treasured blade on his sword-finger) Hah!

The two swords struck one another in mid-air. The Sect Master closed his eyes.

**Sect Master**: Marvellous! That sword of his… there is some inexplicable animate force hidden inside it…

**Long Aotian**: The Sect Master's insight is unmatched! In this Sword Gate challenge, this disciple held out to the second phase of the lightning, to gather as many samples for you as possible…

**Sect Master**: Very good. And her?

**Long Aotian**: Mm. The young lady is well. At present, she is on intimate terms with that strange swordsman.

(The two blades finished their exchange, and fell silent.)

**Sect Master**: (withdrawing his energy, satisfied) Hahaha… it won't be much longer…

**Long Aotian and the envoys**: ………… (holding their reverent posture, unmoving)
