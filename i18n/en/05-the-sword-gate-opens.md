# Chapter Five — The Sword Gate Opens

**Long Aotian**: All this dithering and flinching. Is there no one here who can fight?

A striking man stepped out from behind the envoys, who parted to make way.

**Male Lead**: Wow. Waits for his underlings to finish talking before walking out. What a poser.

**Sword**: Master, you have encountered a rival poser.

**Male Lead**: (glaring at Sword) ……

**Junior Sister**: (to Long Aotian) Right? And who even are you?

**Long Aotian**: Open the Sword Gate and you'll know. No one dares? Fine, then. (He gestures; a subordinate hands him a plain sword.)

**Long Aotian**: Let's practise some calligraphy… (idly beginning to carve characters into the front hall floor)

**Senior Brother**: Stop! This is beyond insult! You — will you fight me yourself?

**Long Aotian**: I will. Receiving my Sword Gate challenge is an honour.

**Senior Brother**: Spare me the condescension! By this sect's Sword Gate — I, Qin Feiyu; sword, Skyplume — OPEN THE SWORD GATE!

**Junior Sister**: Senior Brother!

The ground shook. Wind rose. A strange dimensional space tore open across the sky, and every mind and body present was forced into slow motion.

A repulsive force welled up around Senior Brother, and two pillars of light pierced the horizon. Within one stood the Skyplume Sword, driven straight into the earth.

Senior Brother's eyes glowed red — his consciousness was already locked inside the Skyplume Sword.

**Gate Envoy**: Come, then! (Two men carry forward a sword casket.) I, Long Aotian; sword, Proudheaven — ENTER THE SWORD GATE!

The casket shattered. The Proudheaven Sword was drawn into the second pillar and stood upright in the ground, and Long Aotian's consciousness was locked within it.

**Everyone**: (holding their breath)

The pillars seemed to scan and read the two swords. Light churned through the dimensional space — and then, before each man, a sword was cast out.

Before Long Aotian lay the mirror of the Skyplume Sword. He pulled it free without hesitation and levelled it at Qin Feiyu.

Before Qin Feiyu lay the mirror of the Proudheaven Sword. A flick of his sword-finger turned it toward Long Aotian.

The true duel of the Sword Gate was about to begin.
