# Chapter Fifteen — Sword Gate Echo

**Master**: …Very well. Let this master take you to see it for yourselves — I, Yuan Ping; sword, Origin — **SWORD GATE ECHO. OPEN!**

The main hall shook. Wind roared through it. Pillars and walls warped and melted away, and every person present was transported into a strange space. Cries of alarm went up—

In the distance stood a handsome swordsman and a swordswoman of striking, unworldly grace. Back to back.

**Master**: (looking at the pair in the distance) Yun'er…
