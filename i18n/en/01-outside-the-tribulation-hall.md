# Chapter One — Outside the Tribulation Hall

**Senior Brother**: Ha! If it isn't my junior brother. Your illness worsens by the day!

**Male Lead**: You're the sick one! Your whole family is sick!

**Senior Brother**: Rambling nonsense, muttering to yourself constantly — I told Master long ago that you'd already suffered qi deviation!

**Male Lead**: Is it a crime to talk now?

**Senior Brother**: Junior Brother, don't blame me for being rude. (raising his sword-finger) Master said the cure for qi deviation is to force open the blocked meridians…

**Male Lead**: (sneering) You need an excuse to start a fight?

**Senior Brother**: (finger tensed) Have at you!

Metal rang on metal, sparks scattering — Senior Brother's blade was stopped by an unseen edge of sword-qi.

**Senior Brother**: Ah! Master, when did you arrive?

**Master**: (withdrawing his sword-finger) The Tribulation Hall is not a place for sparring.

**Junior Sister**: (running to Male Lead's side) Right? The qi-field here is delicate. If it draws down the heavenly lightning, who's going to answer for it? Isn't that right, baby?

**Male Lead**: (smug) No cap! Junior Sister, you smell amazing today~

**Junior Sister**: The perfume blend you taught me last time — I really, really like it…

**Senior Brother**: (exploding) Gibberish! Master, look at him — his speech has collapsed into pure disorder, nobody can understand a word of it. This disciple only wishes to clear his meridians!

**Male Lead**: Don't you clear meridians with internal energy? You just used a sword. Bastard move~

**Sword**: Master, that line wasn't cool enough. You should have said — "Senior Brother clears meridians with a sword? He must consider his cultivation superior to Master's own."

**Male Lead**: Superior my ass! Don't drive a wedge between me and Master!

**Senior Brother**: You see? You see! Master! His symptoms grow worse by the day!
