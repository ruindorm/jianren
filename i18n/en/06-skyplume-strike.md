# Chapter Six — Skyplume Strike

The Skyplume Sword within the pillar trembled faintly, answering the red glow in Qin Feiyu's eyes. Holding the mirrored Proudheaven Sword — first strike: **Skyplume Strike**. Target: the Proudheaven Sword in the distant pillar.

Long Aotian raised the mirrored Skyplume to block, but the impact drove him back dozens of paces… and the shockwave set a piercing hum ringing through the dimension.

**Junior Sister**: Senior Brother has improved again! An opening form on pure instinct!

**Sword**: Master! That technique looks designed to counter yours!

**Male Lead**: Feels like a tickle.

**Sword**: Master, do not underestimate him.

**Male Lead**: You say that like you'd win for sure.

**Sword**: Master, according to my historical win-rate analysis, you—

**Male Lead**: (pretending not to hear) Junior Sister, want to sit a little closer? The view's better from here, and the sword-qi won't reach you~

**Sword**: …… (entering spectator mode)

Long Aotian's eyes turned murderous, every thrust aimed at Qin Feiyu's vitals. It seemed he meant to kill the man first, and destroy the Skyplume Sword afterwards.

> **[Elsewhere]**

**Gate Boy**: Wow! What an incredible sight — it's my first time seeing a Sword Gate open!

**Master**: Mm… The Sword Gate is a battle of unconscious instinct. Only the swords themselves act — each one moving its owner's opponent's blade.

**Gate Boy**: What? Then where did Senior Brother go?

**Master**: Your senior brother's consciousness is locked inside the Skyplume Sword. Both men are held in escrow upon the field.

**Gate Boy**: Isn't that terribly dangerous? What if the other side has more people and simply walks in and pulls the sword out?

**Master**: Haha — their own champion is locked in his sword just the same! Look at them: too nervous to move a muscle.

**Gate Boy**: I see! So is Senior Brother asleep right now?

**Master**: Quite the opposite. Your senior brother's consciousness is locked in fierce battle with his opponent, in the space within!

> **[The Inner Space]**

Qin Feiyu settled into a fighting stance. Long Aotian stood with open contempt.

**Long Aotian**: Qin Feiyu. I have a proposal.

**Qin Feiyu**: What scheme are you plotting now?

**Long Aotian**: Let us call a truce here, and let our swords decide the outcome. What say you?

**Qin Feiyu**: (staying on guard) The Sword Gate's verdict rests with the swords regardless. But in here—

**Long Aotian**: You've so little faith in your own blade!

**Qin Feiyu**: Hmph! Fine. Let me see how your sword defeats mine!

**Long Aotian**: (coldly) (And there it is.)

> **[Elsewhere]**

**Gate Boy**: Master, if we can't see the inner space, how is the winner decided?

**Master**: Destroy the opponent's sword within its pillar — or drive it out of the pillar entirely. That wins it.

**Master**: Little disciple… this is a rare lesson for you. Watch closely… make good use of it…

**Gate Boy**: Yes, Master!
