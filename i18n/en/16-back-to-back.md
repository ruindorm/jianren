# Chapter Sixteen — Back to Back

> **[Within the Echo]**

The Handsome Swordsman and the Beautiful Swordswoman stood back to back. Strange light gathered around them. Anyone could see it — the two of them were very afraid.

**Handsome Swordsman**: Yun. Steady your breathing. Don't be frightened. I'm here!

**Beautiful Swordswoman**: I know! I'm here too!

The instant the ninth stage of the Sword Gate descended — the sword in the Beautiful Swordswoman's hands began to glow with an eerie light.

She went white with terror, gripping the hilt with both hands, cold sweat running down her face—

**Beautiful Swordswoman**: Ah! Danger! Ping — run!

**Handsome Swordsman**: What? Wh—

The Beautiful Swordswoman drove her sword into the Handsome Swordsman—
