# Chapter Seventeen — Premonition

The space shifted again. In the distance stood the Handsome Swordsman and the Beautiful Swordswoman — still alive, still whole.

Both of them had just watched it happen, and neither had recovered… The Handsome Swordsman turned to look at her.

**Handsome Swordsman**: Mm. So that is possible. Yun — let me see your sword…

**Beautiful Swordswoman**: Ping, didn't you see it? The ninth-stage premonition — you… you die…

**Handsome Swordsman**: Nobody knows whether a premonition is true. Come. Let me see the sword. We'll find a way to prevent it…

**Beautiful Swordswoman**: No! Ping, we're not doing this. Let's leave…

**Handsome Swordsman**: Don't be absurd! The ninth stage is open — this chance will not come again. Do you not want the catalyst any more?

**Beautiful Swordswoman**: I don't want it.

**Handsome Swordsman**: You—

The space convulsed. The ninth stage of the Sword Gate descended!

And in that same moment—

**Beautiful Swordswoman**: Goodbye, Ping…

**Handsome Swordsman**: NO—!!!
