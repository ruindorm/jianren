# Chapter Fourteen — Asking After the Wounded

**Master**: Feiyu. Wu Ming. Your injuries — are you both well?

**Feiyu**: Thank you for your concern, Master. Your golden elixir works remarkably fast. This disciple is no longer in any danger.

**Wu Ming**: This pill has a sweet aftertaste. It's pretty tasty. Can I have some more?

**Junior Sister**: Senior Brother, that's medicine. If you're hungry I can cook for you…

**Senior Brother**: Master. The Dragonrealm Gate's sword casket — what is it, exactly?

**Master**: That matter… must be told from the beginning. You believe the Sword Gate exists only for duelling? No. It exists so that **two people may pass through it together**.

**Everyone**: What?

**Master**: Nobody knows how many stages there are in total. In my day, I reached only the ninth… Beyond that, the greater the reward, the greater the peril…

**Sword**: Master, please listen properly to the Master's explanation and stop chatting with Junior Sister…

**Wu Ming**: (sighing) Can't you just take notes for me?
